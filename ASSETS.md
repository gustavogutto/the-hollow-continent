# Assets

**Art direction:** Dark Souls 3 / Elden Ring inspired dark fantasy, 2D isometric tactical. Grim desaturated palette: charcoal black (#0b0a08), ash grey, bone (#d6cdbb), ember orange (#e8823c) accents, cold blue (#5a8a9c) for magic. Painterly but clean sharp rendering, bold readable silhouettes.
